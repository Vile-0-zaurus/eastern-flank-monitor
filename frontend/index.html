About

What this is, and what it is not

The app is a weather station. It reads public instruments and shows the picture. It is not the weather service, and it is not a siren. It does not issue an official warning, and it does not tell you what to do.

How it works

It reads stored public coverage and shows attention against the usual level for that country. A missing day or a missing source is no data. It is not zero, and it is not calm.

Data sources

The 2021–22 replay uses stored GDELT headlines. The Now snapshot uses stored GDELT rows, Yle, BBC, and official pages already fetched. Bank of Russia ruble and key-rate files stop in February 2022, so they are not on the Now tab.

Built only from publicly available sources: open datasets (GDELT), public news feeds and official pages. Collected politely and automatically, without bypassing access blocks, logins or paywalls, and respecting site terms. Third-party trackers were used only to discover incidents; every item links to its source. Not an official warning service. In a crisis, follow your national authorities.
