Headline labeling

Labeling guide

Q1. Is this headline about Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, or Poland — not just a passing mention? If no, mark Relevant = no and go to the next headline. Skip Q2.

Q2. If yes, score each frame. 0 = absent. 1 = mild, a hint, or a loaded word. 2 = strong or central.

  • F1 — persecution of Russians or Russian speakers, including “compatriots”
  • F2 — Nazism, fascism, or glorifying Nazis
  • F3 — illegitimate statehood or borders, or “historical Russian lands”
  • F4 — NATO or the West preparing aggression, or militarizing near Russia
  • F5 — Russophobia, or a hostile or discriminating state
  • F6 — threats of a Russian military response or retaliation

Judge the wording, not whether the claim is true and not the politics. Neutral fact reporting is 0. A quote from a Russian official counts. If you are unsure, pick the lower score and write a note. Take a break every 15–20 headlines.
