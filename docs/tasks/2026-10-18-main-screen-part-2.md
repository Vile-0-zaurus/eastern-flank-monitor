## Build

1. Country selector for Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, and Poland. The rest of the main screen filters to the selected country.
2. Per-country reading from headlines that name that country. If fewer than about 20 headlines name it that week, say the reading is uncertain. Map events to countries only as given: Kaliningrad to Lithuania and Poland; Belarus to Lithuania, Latvia, and Poland; northwest Russia to Finland and Estonia.
3. Card text must be concrete, with a number and a comparison, such as how many times more Russian media wrote about that country than usual.
4. Tension meter: three zones, no numbers, a trend arrow. Why: at most two plain causes, or "No new events this week." What it means for you: no action needed, follow official guidance, with a link.
5. This week: at most three events, at most three official statements. One neutral line. Mark the AI summary and the official link. Mark both sides. One "not happening" line when that is true.
6. Recent events under the map: last eight weeks, one line, date, and link.
7. Check it yourself: only official links that were verified to load, per country — crisis or preparedness page, defense ministry, border guard, and NATO. Drop any link that cannot be verified.
8. No-scare rules: no alarm words unless quoting an official source. Numbers need context. Muted colors. No flashing. The self-test scans visible text for banned alarm words.
9. The week is shown at the top, as "Week of 8 November 2021". News, charts, and layers sit behind More.
