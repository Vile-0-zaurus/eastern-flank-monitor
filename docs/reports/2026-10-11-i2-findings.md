# Findings on the first Intent run

Date: 2026-10-11. No model call. No rescore.

Pre-registered results stand unchanged. Cutoffs, weights, the median-and-scaled-MAD baseline, and the stored week levels in docs/reports/2026-10-11-i2-results-intent-scoring.md were not edited. This note is a reading of that table. It is not a new scoring run.

## 1. Eastern flank Intent is a tension detector

The Intent peaks in the stored series line up with the dates named for the Belarus border crises, not with a war on the five flank countries.

1. July–August 2021. Intent reached 3 on the weeks of 2021-07-26 (48 hostile of 200) and 2021-08-02 (33 of 200). Stored hostile titles in the 2021-08-02 week name Lithuania and migrants at the Belarus border.
2. November 2021. Intent reached 3 on the weeks of 2021-11-08 and 2021-11-15, and 4 on 2021-11-22 (49 hostile of 200). Stored hostile titles in those weeks name Poland, Belarus, migrants, and the border.
3. May 2021, the week the pre-registration called the Ryanair/Minsk spike. Week 2021-05-24 has I1 share 0.0209 and I1 level 4. Intent that week is 1, not a peak. I2 was 21 of 200.

No invasion of Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, or Poland follows those peaks inside this window. The registered convergence rule already blocks an overall rise above Watchful while the other layers are empty. That is why a single-layer peak is not enough.

## 2. Averaging hid the Ukraine volume spike

Ukraine I1 share goes from 0.0373 on the week of 2022-01-10 to 0.2731 on the week of 2022-02-21. That is about 4% to 27%. The 27% week is partial (4 in-window days) and was not ranked.

On the ranked February weeks, I1 stays at level 4 (shares 0.0894 and 0.1537) while I2 falls from 0.4000 on 2022-01-17 to 0.2200 on 2022-02-14. Intent on those February weeks is 2, then 1. The weighted average (I1 weight 1, I2 weight 2) pulled the layer down while the volume signal was at its top registered level.

This is not true of every mid-January week. The week of 2022-01-17 is Intent 3, because I2 that week was also level 3 (40 of 100).

## 3. The Ukraine calibration window contains the spring 2021 weeks

The baseline is the 25 full weeks inside 2021-01-01 through 2021-06-30. That window includes the week of 2021-03-22 (I2 43 of 100, Intent 3) and the week of 2021-04-26 (I2 44 of 100, Intent 3). The stored Ukraine I2 median on those 25 weeks is 0.2900. A baseline that includes those weeks sits higher than a window that excluded them would. That alternative was not computed. The registered median stands.

## 4. Ukraine I2 is high in this window, not a measured ceiling since 2014

In the 59 ranked Ukraine weeks, I2 share runs from 0.1500 to 0.4400. Five weeks are at or above 0.4000. The calibration median is 0.2900, against 0.1200 for the eastern flank. That is a high share. It is not near 1.

The stored window starts 2021-01-01. "Hostile since 2014" is not a result of this archive. It was not measured here, and it is not added to the score.

## Planned post-hoc analyses (NOT run, reported separately)

These were not run. They are not a change to the pre-registered results. A later run must be reported on its own, with the registered levels left as they are.

2. Hostile count instead of share. Score the weekly hostile count, not hostile divided by classified. The count panel on the charts is a display of the stored numerator. It is not this analysis.
3. Ukraine alternative calibration window. Refit the Ukraine median and scaled MAD on a window that does not include March–April 2021, and show the new levels beside the registered ones. Do not replace the registered baseline.
