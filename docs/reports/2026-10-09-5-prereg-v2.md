# Pre-registration amendment

Date: 2026-10-09. No model calls. No real-data scoring run.

Tag to follow this commit: `scoring-prereg-v2`.

The first registration, `scoring-prereg`, treated 2021-01-01 through 2021-06-30 as a calm window and used a mean and sample standard deviation. That window contains the week of 2021-05-24. In the stored I1 series that week is a spike (share 0.0209 after the phrase edit, 0.0212 before it). Likely the Ryanair/Minsk week. A mean would treat that spike as part of normal.

The amendment keeps the same cutoffs: 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0. The center is the median. The scale is the median absolute deviation times 1.4826. Weights are unchanged. A zero scale is no data, except when today's value equals the median, which is level 0.

This was not computed from the archive. The spike is the reason for the method change. The median and the scale for the real series were not calculated.
