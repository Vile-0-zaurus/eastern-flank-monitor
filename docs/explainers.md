# Fixed comparisons

Date: 2026-10-11. This is the written set. SPEC §2 says comparisons are written once and reviewed. Use only these, and only on the About page, in level definitions, in the README, and in the portfolio. Do not add a new one here without a review. Daily summaries stay plain and factual. They do not use these lines, and they do not invent others.

A comparison explains a method. It is not a score, and it is not a claim about a week.

## Weather station — the app

The app is a weather station. It reads public instruments and shows the picture. It is not the weather service, and it is not a siren. It does not issue an official warning, and it does not tell you what to do. In a crisis, follow your national authorities.

## Smoke detector and burnt toast — the backtest

A smoke detector should sound for a fire. It should not sound every time someone burns toast. Test A, Ukraine, asks whether the method rises before 24 February 2022. That is the fire check. Test B, the eastern flank, asks whether tension in that window is treated as a war. A bump is burnt toast. A war-level alarm there, with no war in those five countries, is the detector failing that check. The stored results are not changed by this comparison.

## Two witnesses — convergence

One witness is not enough to raise the town alarm. The overall level stays at Watchful until two layers are elevated at the same time. An empty layer is not a second witness. It is also not zero.

## Normal body temperature — the baseline

A temperature is read against what is normal, not against zero. The registered normal is the median of the calibration weeks. The scale is the median absolute deviation times 1.4826. A high week is high relative to that reference. The cutoffs were set before the scoring run. They are not a fever chart drawn after the fact.

## Opinion poll — sampling

I2 does not read every headline. Each week it reads a fixed sample, the way a poll reads a sample of people. The share is an estimate, with a confidence interval. Someone who did not answer is left out. They are not counted as a no, and they are not counted as calm.

## Referee and video replay — the AI judge

The classifier makes the first call. A second model can look again at a flagged sample, the way a referee's call can be checked on replay. The replay does not rewrite the rules. Where a human label exists, that written call stands.

## Sealed envelope — pre-registration

The cutoffs and the baseline method were written down and tagged before the real-data scoring run. That is a sealed envelope. You do not open it after the result and change the rules to fit the score. A later change is a new envelope, and it is reported on its own. The first run's levels stay as stored.

## Silent phone with a dead battery — no data is not calm

A phone that does not ring may have nothing to say, or it may be off because the battery is dead. Silence is not the same as calm. A missing day, a missing source date, or an answer marked AI unavailable is no data. It is not zero, and it is not Calm.
